Catch the Intent. Clarify the Need. Trigger the Action.

Triage raw customer support messages in English, Kannada script, and Romanized Kanglish. Extract critical entities, detect multiple intents, guard personal privacy with automated PII masking, and ask targeted questions when ambiguous.

Launch Intent Detector View Analytics
01

Input & Guard

Raw text is normalized and scanned for PII (cards, phone, email, UPI). Sensitive details are masked; Order IDs are preserved.

02

Classify & Extract

TF-IDF and character n-grams score 10 intent classes. Clause splitting isolates multiple requests; regex extracts amounts, dates & products.

03

Clarify or Route

Uncertain predictions trigger targeted clarifying questions; confident intents route directly to specialist teams with priority tags.

Core Engine Capabilities

🎯

Intelligent Classification

N-gram ML pipeline trained on real conversational phrasings; predicts probabilities across 10 business intents.

🔍

Context-Aware NER

Extracts order numbers, amounts in INR/USD, relative dates, and catalog items from product gazetteers.

🌐

Multilingual & Kanglish

Tolerates script mixing, Kannada script (ಕನ್ನಡ), and colloquial Romanized Kannada without external translation APIs.

🔀

Multi-Intent Clause Splitter

Handles combined requests such as "cancel my order and refund the money" into discrete routed clauses.

🛡️

Automated PII Masking

Luhn-validated credit cards, phone numbers, UPI IDs, and emails masked into [TOKEN] tags before database storage.

🔄

Human Feedback Loop

Low-confidence messages are queued for human review. Reviewer corrections feed into batch retraining runs.
